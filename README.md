# Healthcare Facility Performance Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes a small healthcare dataset to understand patient demographics, healthcare service utilization, and facility visit patterns.

The analysis was developed using Excel, SQL, Power BI, and AI-assisted data analysis techniques.

## Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

- Analyze patient demographics
- Compare healthcare service utilization
- Examine patient visits across healthcare facilities
- Identify patterns in age and gender distribution
- Create an interactive healthcare performance dashboard
- Generate data-driven insights and follow-up questions

## Dataset

The dataset contains healthcare records with the following fields:

- Patient ID
- Age
- Gender
- Facility
- Service
- Visits

The dataset contains 5 patient records and 20 total recorded visits.

## Tools Used

- **Microsoft Excel** — Data preparation and exploratory analysis
- **SQL** — Data querying and aggregation
- **Power BI** — Data visualization and dashboard development
- **AI-assisted analysis** — Insight generation and data storytelling

## Key Analysis

### Patient Distribution by Service

- Family Planning: 2 patients
- ANC: 1 patient
- General: 1 patient
- Immunization: 1 patient

Family Planning recorded the highest number of patients in the dataset.

### Facility Visit Analysis

- PHC B: 11 visits
- PHC A: 5 visits
- PHC C: 4 visits

PHC B recorded the highest number of visits, accounting for 55% of the 20 total visits.

### Gender Distribution

- Female: 4 patients (80%)
- Male: 1 patient (20%)

Female patients represented the larger share of the recorded patients.

### Age Distribution

- 18–24: 1 patient
- 25–34: 3 patients
- 35–44: 1 patient

The 25–34 age group had the highest number of patients.

## Key Insights

1. PHC B recorded the highest visit volume, with 11 of the 20 total visits.
2. The 25–34 age group represented the largest age group in the dataset, with 3 patients.
3. Female patients accounted for 80% of the recorded patients.
4. Family Planning had the highest number of patients among the recorded services.

## Follow-up Questions

Further analysis could investigate:

- What factors are associated with the higher visit volume at PHC B?
- What factors may contribute to the difference in service utilization across the recorded healthcare services?
